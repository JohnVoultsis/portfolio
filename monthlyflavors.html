MonthlyFlavors

MonthlyFlavors was a subscription-based e-commerce service delivering premium Mediterranean food products to customers across Europe. As the founder and full-stack developer, I built the business from the ground up—overseeing branding, logistics, partnerships with local producers, and designing the complete digital experience.

Tasks

  • Research
  • UX UI design
  • Product design
  • Marketing assets
  • Email design
  • Social media posts

Overview

MonthlyFlavors aimed to connect people with authentic Greek and Mediterranean flavors through a subscription model. Customers received carefully curated boxes of artisanal products, along with stories about their origin and producers.

Scrollable mockups

I designed a clean and modern website focused on clarity and usability. The layout highlights a strong call-to-action with a subscription button that stands out visually, guiding users toward conversion. The content strategy emphasizes simplicity, making it easy for visitors to quickly understand the value proposition. Each design choice—from typography to spacing—was aimed at creating a seamless user journey with an engaging visual hierarchy.

Designing a mobile navigation.
The mobile menu was crafted with simplicity and usability in mind. I prioritized the most important actions—Sign up and Subscribe—using bold, contrasting buttons to drive engagement. By minimizing secondary options and focusing on clarity, the menu provides users with an intuitive path, ensuring quick access to essential features even on smaller screens.

Printed experience design.
I designed a custom print for each MonthlyFlavors box to enhance the unboxing experience. The design features a map with the origin locations and detailed information about each product inside. It was printed on thick satin-coated paper with a central crease, so it folds neatly over the products—creating a moment of discovery as customers open the box and reveal the treasures inside.

MonthlyFlavors V2 – A More Organic Redesign

For the second version of MonthlyFlavors, I shifted the focus from a startup-style look to a more organic and approachable experience. The goal was to highlight authenticity and warmth — reflecting the artisanal nature of the products and the human stories behind them.

  • Redesigned the website experience with a cleaner, more intuitive structure to improve navigation and storytelling.
  • Created original illustrations (farmer, delivery, cooking) to replace generic imagery, giving the brand a unique and hand-crafted identity.
  • Refined the e-shop design to make browsing and purchasing easier, while keeping the visual language consistent with the subscription journey.
  • Balanced business goals with emotional design, ensuring the platform felt professional yet personal — closer to the Mediterranean lifestyle.

The redesign gave MonthlyFlavors a stronger brand voice: less like a subscription startup, and more like a curated cultural experience, bringing people closer to Greek producers through design, illustration, and storytelling.

MonthlyFlavors – Packaging & Branding System

To reinforce the artisanal and premium character of MonthlyFlavors, I designed a flexible stamp-based branding system. The goal was to create packaging that felt handcrafted and authentic, while remaining cost-efficient and scalable for logistics.

  • Custom vector stamps. Farmer, olive tree, and logo illustrations used across boxes and wrapping.
  • Scalable design. Neutral boxes branded with stamps instead of costly pre-printing.
  • Human touch. Hand-pressed stamps give each box a unique, personalized feel.
  • Tailored curation. Packaging reflects the idea of hand-picked, carefully prepared products.
  • Digital–physical consistency. Visual identity aligned across packaging and the e-shop.

This system made the packaging flexible, distinctive, and emotionally engaging, turning each delivery into part of the MonthlyFlavors experience.

I expanded MonthlyFlavors’ website with two new subpages: “Our Producers” and “Our Boxes.” These additions build on the V2 homepage redesign, aiming to deepen user engagement for the Greek food subscription service. “Our Producers” hosts interviews with suppliers to foster community and transparency, while “Our Boxes” archives past boxes with interactive maps and product details for discovery and reference. The focus was on creating engaging, informative content that turns passive visitors into active subscribers, emphasizing the “flavorful journey in a single box” narrative.

Targeting both prospects and existing customers, these pages encourage exploration, boosting retention and conversions through storytelling and interactivity.

MonthlyFlavors E-Shop Addition

I integrated an e-shop into MonthlyFlavors’ platform, featuring over 1,400 premium Greek food products. This expansion complements the subscription boxes, allowing direct purchases. The main page includes themed seasonal banners like “Autumn Sale” for engaging promotions beyond simple discounts, while product pages offer detailed info, search by categories, tags (brands, flavors), and certifications (gluten-free, organic, vegan).

Targets food enthusiasts for broader shopping options alongside subscriptions.

Automated Payments & Workflow Integration

To streamline operations and provide a seamless customer experience, I designed and implemented an automated payment and data flow using Stripe and Zapier. This ensured that transactions were secure, customer data was organized, and notifications were instant.

  • Stripe integration: Enabled secure subscriptions and one-time payments with full compliance.
  • Zapier automations: Connected order data to Google Sheets for real-time tracking and reporting.
  • Team notifications: Automatic Slack alerts ensured the team stayed updated on every purchase.
  • Operational efficiency: Reduced manual work, minimized errors, and scaled smoothly with demand.

This system turned the backend into a self-running workflow, where every order triggered a chain of automated tasks — from payment confirmation to internal reporting — keeping the focus on customer experience and growth.

Closing Reflections

Running MonthlyFlavors from 2015 to 2025 was more than just building a business — it was like raising a project that grew with me. From the first website launch to scaling the brand across Europe, every step required me to adapt, learn, and push both my creative and technical limits. I wore many hats: founder, full-stack developer, UX/UI designer, and brand strategist.

What started as an idea turned into a decade-long journey of designing, developing, and delivering authentic Mediterranean experiences to thousands of people’s homes. Along the way, I learned the importance of listening to users, testing ideas in real conditions, and iterating continuously.

Looking back, this project taught me resilience, ownership, and adaptability. It gave me the rare opportunity to shape not just a product, but an entire ecosystem — from packaging and e-commerce to payments, logistics, and customer delight. Despite the challenges, bringing MonthlyFlavors to life and watching it flourish remains one of the most meaningful chapters of my career.
